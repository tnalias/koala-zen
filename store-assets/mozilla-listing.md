## 2. Description

### English
Koala Zen is a gentle, playful break reminder for your browser.

Every 30 minutes, at an interval you choose, an adorable koala appears at the edge of the page. First it invites you to do the habit you want to build, moving by default. Then it comes back to whisper a short piece of wisdom before leaving.

New in 2.0, a cat that simply drops by, sometimes with a fox. It walks across the page, says hello and leaves. Every now and then two passers-by cross paths, two cats, or a cat and a fox, and trade a quick joke. It asks nothing, it counts for nothing, it is there for no reason at all. Five different little scenes, at most one every two hours, only in daylight hours, and never during a call or a pause. Switch it off in one click if you prefer quiet.

Make it yours:
- Set your own habit goals, like "Do 3 push-ups" or "Drink a glass of water". Reorder them by dragging, and the koala shows one at a time, in your order.
- Confirm each break as done or postponed, and note how you feel on a simple 1 to 10 energy scale, so every day counts.
- Follow your progress by day, week, month, year or everything, and move back through past periods. Each figure comes with a line that reads it rather than repeating it, how it compares with the period before, the time of day you postpone most, whether your energy is rising or easing and since when. Per-day charts, your best streak and your all-time record. Everything is kept on your device for as long as you want, with a button to erase it.
- Pick a rhythm that fits: Pomodoro, Deep work, Ultradian, or your own interval, from 5 to 240 minutes in steps of five.
- Snooze or pause when needed, later, one hour, or the rest of the day. Fell behind, catch up your missed breaks.
- Pause automatically during video calls (Infomaniak kMeet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet) and when you step away from the keyboard.
- Turn on an optional focus mode, a break that dims the page until the timer ends. Off by default.
- See your screen time for the day, in the wisdom bubble and in the statistics. It resets at midnight in the time zone you choose (Europe/Zurich by default, or your browser's).
- Add your own images, PNG or animated GIF, up to 18, and write your own quotes one by one or paste a whole batch at once.
- Postpone and one of five characters turns up to count your postponements on a tally, a different one each time, starting over every morning. Refusing is always a valid answer, and it is never punished.
- New in 2.1, back up and restore the same data on your own kDrive (Infomaniak's cloud storage, free account at [infomaniak.com/en/free-cloud](https://www.infomaniak.com/en/free-cloud) if you don't have one yet), completely optional and off by default.

Privacy first, Koala Zen runs entirely on your computer by default. No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking. Your settings and anything you add stay in your browser. The only exception is an optional backup to your own kDrive, which stays off unless you turn it on yourself in the Data tab. The source is public, so you can check it line by line.

Around 60 hand-picked quotes span many voices, from Buddha, Jesus, Rumi, Saadi and Gibran to Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus, Lao Tzu, Nietzsche and Steve Jobs. Hide the ones that do not speak to you, or copy one into your own list.

Interface in English, French and German, following your browser's language.

Open source (MIT license): https://github.com/tnalias/koala-zen

## 5. Privacy & permissions

### Data collection (AMO submission questionnaire)
**Corrected 2026-08-02.** Since 2.0.1 the add-on has an *optional* kDrive backup, so the blanket "no data collection" answer below is no longer accurate on its own — it must be paired with the opt-in caveat, matching `manifest.json`'s `data_collection_permissions` (`required: ["none"]`, `optional: ["authenticationInfo", "personallyIdentifyingInfo"]`).

Answer: **By default, this add-on does NOT collect or transmit any user data and makes no network requests.** It has no analytics. Optionally, if the user pastes their own Infomaniak kDrive access token in the Data tab and connects, the add-on uploads the same data as the local export (settings, goals/quotes, statistics, imported images, depending on what the user ticks) to the user's own kDrive, only when the user clicks "Back up" or restores from there. This is off unless the user turns it on themselves, and disconnecting stops it.

### Privacy policy (ready to paste)
**English:** By default, Koala Zen does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, and makes no network requests to any server. All settings and any content you add (custom images, quotes, reminder messages, chosen time zone, daily screen-time counter) are stored locally in your browser using the standard extension storage, and never leave your device. There is no analytics and no tracking. The one exception is an **optional** backup to your own kDrive (Infomaniak), in the Data tab: if you provide your own kDrive access token and connect, the extension uploads the same data as the local export to your own kDrive, folder `Apps/Koala Zen`, only when you choose to back up or restore. Nothing is sent unless you do this, and you can disconnect at any time. The token is encrypted at rest in the extension's local storage and is never included in the local `.json` export file.

### Permissions justification (for the AMO reviewer notes)
- **alarms**: schedule the periodic reminder at the interval you choose.
- **idle**: detect when you have stepped away from the keyboard, so the koala does not interrupt an empty desk. It only reads the active/idle/locked state, never what you are doing.
- **storage** and **unlimitedStorage**: save your settings and your custom images/quotes locally on your device. `unlimitedStorage` lets custom PNG images exceed the small default quota.
- **tabs**: read the URL of the *active* tab only, so the koala can appear on the current page and can be paused on video-call sites. It does not read your browsing history.
- **scripting**: display the koala on the current page (inject the animation) when needed.
- **Host permission (http/https, all sites)**: the koala can appear on any normal web page you are viewing. This access is also used to match the video-call domains, and (since 2.1.0) to let the background script reach `api.infomaniak.com` for the optional kDrive backup. No page content is read, stored, or sent anywhere.
- **declarativeNetRequest** (new in 2.0.1): required only for the optional kDrive backup. Firefox and Chrome both add an `Origin` header to cross-origin requests from an extension, which the kDrive API rejects with a silent redirect. A single static rule (`rules.json`) strips that one header on requests to `api.infomaniak.com` only — it does not block, redirect, or modify any other request, and does nothing at all unless the user has connected kDrive and triggers a backup or restore.
