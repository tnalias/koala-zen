## 2. Description

### English
Koala Zen is a gentle, playful break reminder for your browser.

Every 30 minutes, at an interval you choose, an adorable koala appears at the edge of the page. First it invites you to move a little. Then it comes back to whisper a short piece of wisdom, and leaves in a different, playful way each time: hopping off, rolling away, or floating up on a balloon.

Make it yours:
- Set your own movement goals, like "Do 3 push-ups". The koala shows one at a time.
- Confirm each break as done or not, and follow your progress in a stats view: today, yesterday, this week, this month, or since you started.
- Note how you feel on a simple 1 to 10 energy scale, shown right next to your exercises.
- Pick a rhythm that fits: Pomodoro, Deep work, Ultradian, or your own interval (1 to 240 minutes).
- Snooze or pause when needed: later, one hour, or the rest of the day. Fell behind? Catch up your missed breaks.
- Pause automatically during video calls (Infomaniak kMeet, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet) and when you step away from the keyboard.
- Turn on an optional focus mode: a full-screen break that blocks the page until the timer ends. Off by default.
- See your screen time for the day, shown in the wisdom bubble and in the gentle nudge if you skip a break. It resets at midnight in the time zone you choose (Europe/Zurich by default, or your browser's).
- Add your own koala images (PNG or animated GIF) and write your own quotes.

Privacy first: Koala Zen runs entirely on your computer. No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking. Your settings and anything you add stay in your browser.

Around 60 hand-picked quotes span many voices, from Buddha, Jesus, Rumi, Saadi and Gibran to Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus, Lao Tzu, Nietzsche and Steve Jobs. Every one of them is editable.

Open source (MIT license): https://github.com/tnalias/koala-zen

## 5. Privacy & permissions

### Data collection (AMO submission questionnaire)
Answer: **This add-on does NOT collect or transmit any user data.**
It makes no network requests and has no analytics.

### Privacy policy (ready to paste)
**English:** Koala Zen does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It makes no network requests to any server. All settings and any content you add (custom images, quotes, reminder messages, chosen time zone, daily screen-time counter) are stored locally in your browser using the standard extension storage, and never leave your device. There is no analytics and no tracking.

**Français :** Koala Zen ne collecte, ne stocke ni ne transmet aucune donnée personnelle. L'extension ne fait aucune requête réseau vers un serveur. Tous les réglages et tout contenu ajouté (images, citations, messages personnalisés, fuseau horaire choisi, compteur de temps d'écran du jour) sont stockés localement dans le navigateur via le stockage standard des extensions, et ne quittent jamais l'appareil. Aucune analyse, aucun suivi.

### Permissions justification (for the AMO reviewer notes)
- **alarms**: schedule the periodic reminder at the interval you choose.
- **idle**: detect when you have stepped away from the keyboard, so the koala does not interrupt an empty desk. It only reads the active/idle/locked state, never what you are doing.
- **storage** and **unlimitedStorage**: save your settings and your custom images/quotes locally on your device. `unlimitedStorage` lets custom PNG images exceed the small default quota.
- **tabs**: read the URL of the *active* tab only, so the koala can appear on the current page and can be paused on video-call sites. It does not read your browsing history.
- **scripting**: display the koala on the current page (inject the animation) when needed.
- **Host permission (http/https, all sites)**: the koala can appear on any normal web page you are viewing. This access is also used to match the video-call domains. No page content is read, stored, or sent anywhere.
